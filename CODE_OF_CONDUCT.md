## ⛏️ The Obamium Progression System

We have designed the mod to feel like a natural extension of Minecraft. Here is how you climb the Obamium tech tree.

### Phase 1: The Mining Expedition
Obamium ore spawns exclusively in the deepest depths of the Overworld (Y-level -50 and below). It is incredibly rare, glowing with a mysterious prismatic energy. You will need a Netherite pickaxe to mine it, and even then, the ore is fiercely guarded by the mythical **Obamium Golem**.

### Phase 2: The Forge
Once you have collected enough raw Obamium, you must smelt it in a special **Cosmic Furnace**. The furnace requires a specific combination of lava and soul fire to reach the extreme temperatures needed to melt the metal. The resulting Obamium Ingots are used to craft the tools and armor.

### Phase 3: The Ascension
The ultimate goal of the mod is to craft the **Orb of Obamium**. This legendary item, when held, grants the player permanent Night Vision, Fire Resistance, and a custom buff called "Presidential Aura." It is the ultimate survival tool.

## 🧠 Under The Hood: How The Meme Mod Works

Creating a meme mod that is actually fun requires careful game design. We didn't want the Obamium tools to be so overpowered that they break the game (although the sword does deal 20 damage).

The progression system is gated behind the **Boss Fight**. To get Obamium, you must defeat the Obamium Golem. The Golem has a custom AI model that uses the vanilla Iron Golem behavior but adds a ranged attack that shoots "Executive Orders" (small, glowing projectiles). This makes the fight challenging and rewarding.

The texture work is also a highlight. The Obamium items have a subtle animated shimmer effect. We achieved this using Minecraft's built-in animated texture engine. The armor, when worn, adjusts the player's model to include a custom cape and a glowing aura particle effect.
